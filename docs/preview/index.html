FruitPunch phase 1 review

Balls and tomatoes are thrown at the G1 from close, medium and long range. The robot fits a parabola to the ball in flight, predicts when and where it hits, and checks candidate reactions with the kinematic planner before committing. In DODGE it gets out of the way. In HIT it meets the ball with a hand or a foam bat, using kplanner punches and hooks aimed with arm IK.

    Reaction moves

    Every move starts from kplanner motion. Moves marked "layer" add joint offsets on top of the planner's motion.

    ObjectiveMoves, in the order tried
    DODGEStop · torso twist (layer) · forward lean (layer) · duck at squat 0.3 or 0.2, with optional lean or twist · one-leg kneel · kneel · turn and walk out · crouch-walk out · sidestep · back off · turn. Chest-high and higher throws try ducks before travelling moves; on SONIC, turn-and-walk comes before sidesteps
    HITBoxing guard facing the ball, then a kplanner left or right punch or hook, timed by delaying the mode switch and aimed at full extension with arm IK (layer). Fallback: an arm-IK swat, then hold the guard
